One thing first

A phone photo of a textbook has a shadow across it, a curved page and decorated letters. On-device OCR mangles all three — that is where garbled words come from. Claude reads the photo itself and gets it right. That needs your own Anthropic API key.

Get one at console.anthropic.com → API keys. It is stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic. A page costs well under a cent.

Offline reading needs no key and no internet, but on a page like yours it will get words wrong.
